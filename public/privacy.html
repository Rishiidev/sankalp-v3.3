Privacy Policy

Hanuman Sadhana stores your practice data, journal entries, uploaded audio, and preferences locally in your browser using IndexedDB.

Data Collection

The app does not require an account and does not intentionally send your personal practice data to a server.

Local Storage

Data remains on your device unless you export it or clear your browser/app storage. Uploaded audio files may use significant local storage.

External Services

If you play YouTube links or bundled remote audio, those third-party services may receive standard network request information according to their own privacy policies.
